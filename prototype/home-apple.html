Rates app: interactive prototype

Built with Apple's motion rules: instant response, spring animations, and things you can grab mid-movement. All the numbers are made up.

  1. Drag across the big chart to scrub through prices
  2. Tap دلار or بیت‌کوین under the chart: the chart morphs
  3. Switch روز / هفته / ماه / سال
  4. Switch tabs: ارز · رمزارز · طلا و سکه · نقره
  5. Tap a green or red badge to switch between % and toman
  6. Tap a row to open the detail sheet. Flick it down to close it, or grab it while it's moving
  7. Scroll: the big title moves into the bar
  8. Prices tick every few seconds (simulated)

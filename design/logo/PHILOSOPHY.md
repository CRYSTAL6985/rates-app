# Minted Light

One gesture, struck like a coin. A mark should read in a glance at the size of a fingernail and still reward a close look at full scale: the weight sits low and heavy like metal in the hand, the thin strokes rise like breath.

Material is honest: gold that behaves like gold — bright where it faces the light, deep amber where it turns away — set on warm darkness, never flat black. Light comes from one place, above and slightly left, and every highlight agrees with it.

Space is generous. The mark lives inside the inner two-thirds so any mask — square, squircle, circle — leaves it untouched. Nothing decorative survives that does not also carry meaning.

The quiet reference: the dot of the letter is the last price on a live chart — the small glowing point the eye checks first.

Every curve is drawn, measured, and redrawn: a meticulously crafted object, the product of patient refinement rather than a quick effect.
